# Lexora AI Tutor - User Guide

## Overview

The **AI English Tutor** is an intelligent, bilingual (English & Bengali) learning companion integrated into Lexora. It helps you:
- Understand grammar concepts
- Correct sentences and get feedback
- Practice with AI-guided lessons
- Learn at your own pace

## Accessing the Tutor

1. **Open Lexora** → https://fahim23221.github.io/VX.V1
2. **Log in** with your email/phone + password
3. **Click the "Tutor" tab** in the top navigation

## Features

### 1. Ask Grammar Questions

**Ask about any English concept:**
- "What is the past tense?"
- "How do I use 'would'?"
- "What's the difference between 'been' and 'being'?"

**The tutor will:**
- Explain in simple, beginner-friendly language
- Give you examples
- Suggest practice sentences

**Language:** Select Bengali (বাংলা) or English before asking.

### 2. Correct Your Sentences

1. **Type a sentence** you've written in English
2. **Click "Check with AI"**
3. **Get feedback:**
   - ✓ If correct, tutor will praise and explain the grammar used
   - ✗ If incorrect, tutor will show the mistake, explain why, and provide the correct version

**Example:**
```
You wrote: "I are going to school"
Tutor says: "Almost! 'I' uses 'am', not 'are'. 
            Correct: 'I am going to school.'
            Remember: I + am, You/We/They + are, He/She/It + is"
```

### 3. Interactive Lessons

**Learn one concept at a time:**
- Start with a simple grammar topic
- Practice with guided examples
- Get instant feedback on your answers
- Move to the next concept

### 4. Save & Review

- All conversations are **automatically saved** to your account
- **Access anytime** from any device (after logging in)
- Build a **personal learning history**

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **Do:**
- Ask about one concept at a time
- Give the tutor complete sentences
- Try to write your own sentences before asking for help
- Use corrections as teaching moments

❌ **Avoid:**
- Copying homework answers directly
- Asking multiple unrelated questions at once
- Using slang or very casual text

## Privacy & Security

- Your conversations are **only visible to you**
- Data is stored securely in Firebase
- No conversation data is shared publicly
- Your API usage doesn't cost you anything (paid by Lexora)

## Limitations

- **Rate limit:** 10 requests per minute per user
- **Max input:** 500 characters per message
- **Availability:** Requires internet connection and active Lexora account

## Example Conversations

### Example 1: Grammar Question (English)
**You:** "What is the present perfect tense?"

**Tutor:**
"Present perfect connects the past to the present.

**Structure:** Have/Has + Past Participle

**Examples:**
- I have **studied** English for 2 years.
- She has **lived** in Dhaka since 2020.

**When to use:**
- Something started in the past and continues now
- Recently completed actions with present relevance

Try this: Write a sentence about something you've done today!"

### Example 2: Grammar Question (Bengali)
**আপনি:** "Present Perfect কী?"

**Tutor:**
Present Perfect হল এমন একটি টেনস যা past এবং present এর সংযোগ।

**গঠন:** Have/Has + অতীত বিশেষণ (Past Participle)

**উদাহরণ:**
- I **have studied** English (আমি ইংরেজি পড়েছি—এখনও পড়ছি)
- She **has lived** in Dhaka (তিনি ঢাকায় থাকেন)

এখন নিজে একটি বাক্য তৈরি করে দেখো!

### Example 3: Sentence Correction (English)
**You:** "He go to school every day."

**Tutor:**
"Almost right! 'Go' should be 'goes' with 'he'.

✓ **Correct:** He **goes** to school every day.

**Grammar rule:** 
With he/she/it, add 's' or 'es' to the verb in simple present.
- I go / You go / He goes / She goes / It goes / We go / They go

Great sentence structure though! Practice more and you'll nail this! 💪"

### Example 4: Sentence Correction (Bengali)
**আপনি:** "He go to school every day."

**Tutor:**
"সঠিক নয়! 'He' এর সাথে 'goes' ব্যবহার করতে হবে, শুধু 'go' নয়।

✓ **সঠিক:** He **goes** to school every day.

**ব্যাকরণ নিয়ম:**
He/She/It এর সাথে verb এর শেষে 's' বা 'es' যোগ করতে হয়।
- I go / You go / He goes / She goes / They go

আরও চেষ্টা করো, আপনি পারবে! 💪"
